
Health Insurance Policy - Sample

1. Coverage:
This policy covers hospitalization expenses due to illness, disease, or accidental injury. Coverage includes room rent, ICU charges, doctor's consultation, surgical expenses, and pre and post-hospitalization costs (30 days prior and 60 days post discharge).

2. Exclusions:
The policy does not cover cosmetic treatments, self-inflicted injuries, treatment for alcohol or drug abuse, or injuries resulting from adventure sports.

3. Claim Process:
To initiate a claim, contact the insurer within 24 hours of hospitalization. Submit the required documents including the hospital bill, doctor's prescription, and diagnostic reports.

4. Waiting Period:
A 30-day waiting period is applicable from the policy start date except in case of accidents. Specific diseases like hernia, cataract, and joint replacement have a waiting period of 2 years.

5. Renewal:
The policy is renewable for life. A grace period of 30 days is allowed for premium payment. Claims during the grace period are not covered.

6. Sum Insured:
The sum insured under this policy ranges from ₹2,00,000 to ₹10,00,000. No-claim bonus of 5% is added for every claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50%.

For detailed terms and conditions, refer to the complete policy document.
