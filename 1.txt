1. AGREEMENT
We, UltraShield™ Insurance Exchange (hereinafter "we," "us," or "Company"), in consideration of the premium and subject to all terms of this policy, agree with you, the Named Insured shown in the Declarations, to provide the insurance described herein.

================================================================================
2. DEFINITIONS
Throughout this policy, "you" and "your" refer to the Named Insured and spouse if resident of the same household. "We," "us," and "our" refer to UltraShield™ Insurance Exchange and any affiliated reinsurers.

a. "Residence Premises" – The one-family dwelling principally occupied by you at the address shown in the Declarations.  
b. "Sudden and Accidental" – An event that occurs within a single 60-minute window, is not preceded by any warning signs you should have noticed, and is reported by you within 12 hours.  
c. "Guaranteed Replacement Cost" – The actual cost to repair or replace, up to the Coverage A limit shown in the Declarations, minus depreciation we deem reasonable, subject to Section 7-B.

================================================================================
3. PROPERTY COVERAGES
Coverage A – Dwelling: Up to $500,000.  
Coverage B – Other Structures: 10% of Coverage A.  
Coverage C – Personal Property: Up to $250,000 worldwide, subject to Special Limits.  
Coverage D – Loss of Use: 12 months actual loss sustained or 20% of Coverage A, whichever is less.

================================================================================
4. PERILS INSURED AGAINST
We insure against direct physical loss to property caused by:  
- Fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism, theft, and "other sudden and accidental direct physical losses," except as excluded.

================================================================================
5. EXCLUSIONS
We do not cover:  
a. Earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, sinkhole, soil expansion, or vibration from Zumba classes.  
b. Water damage from flood, surface water, or sewer backup unless the optional "WaterGuard™" endorsement is purchased.  
c. Mold, rot, or fungi unless the optional "HealthyHome" endorsement is purchased.  
d. Cyber-attack, cyber-fraud, loss of cryptocurrency keys.  
e. Nuclear hazard or war, including declared or undeclared cyber-war.  
f. Business property, rental, or home-sharing activity unless disclosed and premium adjusted.

================================================================================
6. DEDUCTIBLE
Standard Deductible: $2,500 each loss.  
Storm Shield Deductible: 2% of Coverage A or $5,000, whichever is greater, applies to all wind or hail losses.  
If the National Weather Service issues a Severe Thunderstorm Watch anywhere in your state on the date of loss, the Storm Shield deductible triples.

================================================================================
7. SPECIAL LIMITS OF LIABILITY
- Jewelry, watches, furs: $1,500 combined.  
- Electronics over three years old: settled at garage-sale value.  
- Cash, bullion, NFTs: $200 aggregate.  
- Food spoilage: $50 max, only if you were hosting a documented dinner party for six or more guests at the moment of power failure.

================================================================================
8. CONDITIONS
8.1 Duties After Loss  
You must:  
a. Notify us via the UltraShield™ app within 12 hours.  
b. Protect property from further damage.  
c. Provide sworn proof of loss, original receipts, and three independent repair estimates within 10 days.  
d. Submit to Examination Under Oath if requested.

8.2 Our Options  
We may:  
a. Repair or replace with material of like kind and quality, or  
b. Pay the actual cash value, or  
c. Declare the structure a "constructive total loss" and pay the policy limit minus deductible.

8.3 Cancellation  
We may cancel for any reason with 10 days' notice in the first 60 days. After 60 days we may cancel or non-renew if you file two or more claims in any three-year period.

8.4 Arbitration  
Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Delaware under Delaware law. You waive class-action rights and jury trial.
