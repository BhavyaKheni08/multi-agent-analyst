3. DEFINITIONS
===============================================================================

"Accident" means a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by 
external, visible and violent means.

"Burglary" means an act involving the unauthorised and forcible entry into 
the vehicle by breaking and entering with felonious intent.

"Certificate of Insurance" means the certificate required to be issued under 
Section 147 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, showing that the vehicle is 
covered by a contract of insurance as required by Chapter XI of the said Act.

"Depreciation" means the decrease in value of parts/accessories due to usage, 
wear and tear, and passage of time, as per the schedule specified in this 
policy.

"Electric Vehicle" means a motor vehicle that is propelled solely by 
electrical power derived only from an electrical rechargeable battery which 
is charged externally.

"Flood" means the overflowing of water from its natural or artificial 
confines such as rivers, streams, drains, reservoirs, tanks, canals, lakes, 
dams.

"Garage" means a structurally covered parking place which is used normally 
for the parking of the motor vehicle when not in use.

"IDV (Insured's Declared Value)" means the maximum sum insured for the 
vehicle as agreed between the insurer and the insured at the time of policy 
inception/renewal, representing the approximate current market value.

"Market Value" means the cost of a similar vehicle of same make, model, type, 
year of manufacture in the same locality or the listed price of such vehicle 
as published by the manufacturers less depreciation.

"Motor Vehicle/Vehicle" means the vehicle specified in the Schedule including 
its standard accessories as fitted by the manufacturers.

"Network Garage" means the garage which has been empanelled by the Company 
for providing cashless claim settlement facility.

"Own Damage" means loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by perils 
covered under Section I of this policy.

"Personal Effects" means goods normally worn, used or carried by individuals 
such as clothing, cameras, sports equipment etc., but excluding money, 
securities, jewellery, and business goods.

"Theft" means the act of stealing as defined under the Indian Penal Code.

"Third Party" means any person other than the insured or any person who is 
driving the insured vehicle with the permission of the insured.

"Total Loss" means:
- Actual Total Loss: The vehicle is completely destroyed or damaged beyond 
  repair
- Constructive Total Loss: Cost of repair exceeds 75% of the IDV

4. INSURING AGREEMENTS
===============================================================================

SECTION I - OWN DAMAGE COVERAGE

Subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions and limitations of this policy, 
the Company will indemnify the Insured against loss or damage to the vehicle 
insured caused by:

a) Fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning
b) Burglary, house-breaking, theft or larceny
c) Riot and strike (subject to special conditions as defined in Motor Tariff)
d) Earthquake (fire and shock damage)
e) Flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, hailstorm, 
   frost
f) Accidental external means
g) Malicious act
h) Terrorist activity (subject to special conditions as defined in Motor 
   Tariff)
i) Transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator or air
j) Landslide/rockslide

The Company will at its option:
- Repair the vehicle at an authorized garage, or
- Replace what is lost or damaged, or
- Pay in cash the amount of loss or damage

Maximum Liability: The Company's liability shall not exceed the IDV specified 
in the Schedule (Rs. 6,50,000).

SECTION II - THIRD PARTY LIABILITY

The Company will indemnify the Insured against all sums which the Insured 
shall become legally liable to pay in respect of:

a) Death of or bodily injury to any person (except death or bodily injury to 
   any employee of the Insured arising out of and in the course of employment)

b) Damage to property belonging to any third party

Coverage includes:
- Compensation payable to third parties
- Legal costs incurred in defending claims (with Company's consent)
- Emergency treatment costs as required under Motor Vehicles Act

Maximum Liability:
- For death/injury to persons: Unlimited as per Motor Vehicles Act
- For property damage: Rs. 7,50,000

SECTION III - PERSONAL ACCIDENT COVER FOR OWNER-DRIVER

The Company will pay compensation as specified in the Schedule to the Insured 
or his/her legal representatives in the event of:

a) Death: 100% of Sum Insured (Rs. 15,00,000)
b) Permanent Total Disablement: 100% of Sum Insured (Rs. 15,00,000)
c) Permanent Partial Disablement: Percentage as per scale in policy

caused by bodily injury directly resulting from an accident to the vehicle.

SECTION IV - ADDITIONAL BENEFITS

A. Emergency Transportation and Hotel Expenses
   Reimbursement up to Rs. 2,500 for accommodation if vehicle becomes 
   non-driveable more than 50 km from residence

B. Emergency Medical Expenses
   Coverage as required under Section 161 of Motor Vehicles Act

C. Loss of Personal Effects
   Coverage up to Rs. 5,000 for personal effects damaged in an accident

5. EXCLUSIONS
===============================================================================

GENERAL EXCLUSIONS

The Company shall not be liable for:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL LOSS: Any consequential loss howsoever caused

2. WEAR AND TEAR: Normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration, mechanical or 
   electrical breakdown, failures or breakages

3. DEPRECIATION: Any depreciation in the value of the vehicle

4. DRIVING WITHOUT VALID LICENSE: Loss or damage caused while the vehicle is 
   being driven by a person who does not hold an effective driving license 
   for the type of vehicle being driven

5. INFLUENCE OF INTOXICATING SUBSTANCE: Loss or damage caused while the 
   vehicle is being driven under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs

6. USE FOR RACING: Loss or damage caused while the vehicle is being used for 
   racing, pace-making, reliability trials, speed testing or any contest

7. WAR RISKS: War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, 
   rebellion, revolution, insurrection, mutiny or usurped power

8. NUCLEAR RISKS: Nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation or radioactive 
   contamination

9. UNLAWFUL USE: Loss or damage caused while the vehicle is being used 
   otherwise than in accordance with the limitations as to use specified

10. GEOGRAPHIC LIMITS: Loss or damage occurring outside the geographical area 
    of India

11. OVERLOADING: Damage caused due to overloading beyond the manufacturer's 
    specifications

12. CONTRACTUAL LIABILITY: Any liability under any contract or agreement 
    unless such liability would have attached in the absence of such contract

13. USE OF VEHICLE AS TOOL: Damage caused while the vehicle is used as a tool 
    of trade

14. DISHONEST ACTS: Loss or damage caused by theft or conversion by any person 
    in lawful possession of the vehicle

15. ELECTRICAL/ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT: Damage to electrical/electronic fittings 
    due to short circuit unless consequent fire damage occurs

SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS FOR SECTION I (OWN DAMAGE)

1. Damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time
2. Loss of personal belongings except as specifically covered
3. Damage while in custody of motor dealer for sale
4. Damage due to overturning unless caused by accidental external means

SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS FOR SECTION II (THIRD PARTY LIABILITY)

1. Death/injury to driver of the insured vehicle
2. Damage to insured vehicle or property in custody of insured
3. Liability assumed under contract unless would exist without contract
4. Punitive or exemplary damages

6. GENERAL CONDITIONS
===============================================================================

1. CONDITION PRECEDENT TO LIABILITY
The due observance and fulfillment of the terms, provisions, conditions and 
exclusions of this policy by the Insured shall be conditions precedent to 
any liability of the Company.

2. DUTY TO TAKE REASONABLE PRECAUTIONS
The Insured shall take all reasonable steps to safeguard the vehicle from 
loss or damage and maintain it in efficient condition. The vehicle must 
possess a valid registration certificate, permit, fitness certificate and 
pollution under control certificate as required by law.

3. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

A. Immediate Requirements:
- Report to police within 24 hours if involving third party or theft
- Inform the Company within 7 days of the incident
- File FIR in case of theft, riot, strike, malicious damage

B. Documentation Required:
- Duly completed claim form
- Original policy documents
- Registration certificate copy
- Driving license copy
- Police FIR/Non-FIR/Panchnama (if applicable)
- Repair estimates/bills
- Survey reports if appointed

C. Claims Settlement:
- Cashless facility available at network garages
- Survey will be conducted for claims above Rs. 20,000
- Settlement within 30 days of receipt of all documents

4. RIGHT OF RECOVERY (SUBROGATION)
In the event of any payment being made under this policy, the Company shall 
be subrogated to all rights of recovery of the Insured and may pursue such 
rights in the name of the Insured.

5. CANCELLATION

By Insured:
- 15 days written notice required
- Premium refund as per short period scale
- No refund if claim has been made

By Company:
- 15 days written notice required
- Full refund of proportionate premium
- May cancel immediately for fraud or material misrepresentation

6. RENEWAL
This policy shall terminate on the expiry date unless renewed. The Company 
reserves the right to refuse renewal or modify terms at renewal.

7. ARBITRATION
All disputes arising under this policy shall be referred to arbitration in 
accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitration 
shall be held at the place where the policy was issued.

8. TERRITORIAL LIMITS
This policy covers the vehicle only within the geographical limits of India.

9. MULTIPLE INSURANCE
If there is any other insurance covering the same risk, the Company will not 
be liable for more than its rateable proportion of the loss.

10. FRAUD
If any claim is fraudulent or if fraudulent means are used to obtain benefit 
under this policy, all benefits shall be forfeited and the policy shall 
become void.

11. JURISDICTION
All legal proceedings shall be subject to the jurisdiction of Indian courts 
only.

12. SURVEY AND ASSESSMENT
The Company has the right to have any loss surveyed and assessed. No 
settlement shall be made without such survey if the loss exceeds Rs. 20,000.
